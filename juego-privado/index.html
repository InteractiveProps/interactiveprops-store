You are standing on the edge of the cliff. The only way across is the one you build.

  • 1Pick up donuts by walking over them: they stack on your head.
  • 2Walk toward the chasm and each donut turns into a bridge plank.
  • 3Fill every gap to reach the far platform. Step into the void and you go back to the start.
  • 4There are 10 levels: each one with a longer chasm, a narrower bridge and a different shape.
  • WASD↑↓←→Move (or drag on screen)
  • =+1 donut on the stack
  • −−1 donut off the stack
  • XDump the whole stack
  • RTear the bridge down
  • IMagnet: 20 s pulling donuts in (stacks up)
  • BDance: 15 s emote (stacks up, cannot be cut)
  • NRestart the current level
